### Funnel Performance

The weakest segment is **Mobile Web / Leisure Occasional**, converting at **17.86%**.

### Customer Value

The highest-value customer segment is **Frequent Business / Gold**, contributing **GBP 1,539,058.74** in platform revenue.

### Route Performance

The highest-revenue route is **RailPartner Station 034 to RailPartner Station 008**, producing **GBP 27,534.26** in platform revenue.

### Operator Performance

The strongest operational performer is **Southern Coast Rail**, with disruption at **11.09%**.

### Refund Exposure

The largest refund-related commercial exposure is **Full Refund / Operator Cancellation**, representing **GBP 2,848,912.09** in value at risk.

## Recommended Actions

1. Protect and grow high-revenue strategic partners.
2. Investigate low-conversion channels and customer segments.
3. Prioritise high-demand routes with elevated disruption.
4. Incorporate reliability metrics into partner scorecards.
5. Target high-value customer segments with retention initiatives.
6. Monitor refund exposure as a commercial KPI.
7. Test product changes using measurable experimentation frameworks.
